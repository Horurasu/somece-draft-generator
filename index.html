01About the project

The SOMECE Draft Generator is an instructional design artifact that frames generative AI as a mirror rather than a typewriter.

The tool does not embed a language model, nor does it generate text directly. Instead, it structures the author–AI relationship through a nine-step itinerary, mandatory prior writing, feedback-oriented prompts (not substitution), and a self-regulation checklist with a level indicator.

Its aim is to preserve judgment, authorship, and critical thinking in academic writing: it shifts cognitive load toward secondary tasks such as rephrasing, tone, and organization, but never toward the construction of the central judgment.

This repository does not contain the working application, which is no longer available, but the design documentation that allows readers to verify the architecture of the artifact as analyzed in the article. The full analysis lives in the article itself.

  1. WriteThe author drafts each section in their own words first.
  2. ComposeThe tool turns that text into a prompt asking for feedback, not a finished answer.
  3. AskThe author takes the prompt to the AI platform of their choice.
  4. ReworkWhat comes back is treated as a draft, to be revised by the author.
